Tell your story simply

Doug Cirillo joins host Brian Sheehy on Determination, a podcast about the search for work and the persistence it takes. The conversation runs from distilling what you do into a story people can actually hire, through digital product, marketing and customer experience, to the simplest test of the work: are we bringing joy to our customers?
