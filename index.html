About

I build digital and data functions at audience brands, then run them.

I am Head of Digital Experience and Growth at Vanguard, where I lead a function supporting Personal Wealth marketing and the retail business. Before Vanguard, I led marketing and product strategy at Gangverk, was Vice President of Digital Product Management at A Place for Mom, directed digital product at Lindblad Expeditions, ran digital product and the growth stack at Sotheby’s, and built and ran digital product at Madison Square Garden.

The pattern across those seats is consistent: build with the people who will run it, put the data foundation first, measure against outcomes, and leave something that still stands after you leave the room. Former leaders have recruited me back three times.

I write Observations from the Middle Seat. My degree is a BA in Journalism from Susquehanna University, with an MBA from Seton Hall. I started college on a computer science track and switched when the dot-com bubble burst, which explains a career spent half in the build and half in the story.
