About

I build digital and data functions at audience brands, then run them.

I am Head of Digital Experience and Growth at Vanguard, where I lead a function supporting Personal Wealth marketing and the retail business. Before Vanguard, I led marketing and product strategy at Gangverk, was Vice President of Digital Product Management at A Place for Mom, directed digital product at Lindblad Expeditions, ran digital product and the growth stack at Sotheby’s, and built and ran digital product at Madison Square Garden.

The pattern across those seats is consistent: build with the people who will run it, put the data foundation first, measure against outcomes, and leave something that still stands after you leave the room. Former leaders have recruited me back three times.

I write Observations from the Middle Seat(opens in new tab). My degree is a BA in Journalism from Susquehanna University, with an MBA from Seton Hall. I started college on a computer science track and switched when the dot-com bubble burst, which explains a career spent half in the build and half in the story.

Lindblad Expeditions

Digital product leader

The experience had not kept pace with the business: a slow, fragmented site, and no clear view of how customers moved from browsing to booking. The approach was a composable replatform, run by a team of 10 across product, analytics, design, and partners, on a $5M portfolio.

  • Bookings up 30% and booking funnel completion up 82%, within eight months of the replatform
  • Qualified leads up 40%
  • The rebuilt expeditions.com was named a Webby Honoree

Sotheby’s

Assistant Vice President, Director of Digital Product and Growth Stack · 2017–2020

Search was not carrying its weight, and the customer data underneath was fragmented across systems, so search, personalization, and measurement all worked from fragments. The approach was a rearchitecture: off Adobe Experience Manager, onto a composable, data-first stack.

  • Organic traffic up 167%
  • On-site search click-through up 48.7%
  • Consignment landing pages doubled search placement and cut consignment ad spend 50%
  • The data and measurement foundations kept supporting the business after his departure

Madison Square Garden

Director of Product Operations · 2012–2016

Every brand ran its own digital property on its own aging platform: the Knicks, the Rangers, the Liberty, the Rockettes, Radio City Music Hall, and the arena itself. The approach was to build one digital product function and one operating model across all of them, including a $4M replatform he proposed and led.

  • 29+ websites and 4+ native apps under one operating model
  • The MSG GO app, New York Emmy-nominated
  • Digital ticket sales up 400% for the Rockettes and Radio City Music Hall
